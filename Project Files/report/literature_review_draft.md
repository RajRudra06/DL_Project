## Comparison paragraph (draft)
Early work treated opinion spam with hand-built features and linear models [1], [2]; Jindal and Liu found that review text alone gave only 63% AUC, while Ott et al. reached 89.8% on a small crowd-written corpus. Neural models then replaced hand-made features: Ren and Zhang's CNN-plus-gated-RNN-with-attention beat feature-based methods in three domains [4], building on the sentence CNN of Kim [3]. The target has since changed from human-written to machine-written fakes. Salminen et al. showed that fine-tuned RoBERTa separates GPT-2 reviews from real ones at 97% while people score 55% [6], and 2025 work reports 97 to 98% with DistilBERT and DeBERTa on ChatGPT-rephrased and GPT-2 data [8], [9]. Those studies fine-tune one transformer and rarely compare it with shallower architectures under one protocol, and their datasets differ in size and source, so the numbers cannot be compared directly. Zero-shot detectors [7] avoid training data but need the generator's probabilities, and graph methods [10] need reviewer and product links. Our project fills the gap: four architecture families, one dataset, one split and one metric set, with parameter count and latency reported next to accuracy.
